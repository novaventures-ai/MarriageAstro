# Life partner kaisa hoga: what a chart can and cannot say about a spouse

## What is actually knowable

A chart does not name a person. It describes a *type* — a cluster of
temperament, appearance, background and circumstance that the person is likely
to be drawn to and likely to end up with. Read honestly, it is closer to a
personality sketch than a photograph, and it is most accurate about character
and least accurate about physical description.

Five independent indicators describe the spouse. They are read together, and
where they disagree the disagreement is itself information — it usually means
the person is pulled between two types.

## 1. The 7th house and its lord

The sign on the **7th house** sets the baseline. Its element does most of the
work:

- **Fire** (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) — direct, energetic, independent, wants
  to lead
- **Earth** (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) — practical, stable, financially
  careful, slow to commit and slow to leave
- **Air** (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) — verbal, social, intellectually
  restless, needs conversation
- **Water** (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) — emotionally intense, intuitive,
  private, forms deep and difficult-to-break attachments

Planets **sitting in** the 7th modify this strongly and often override the
sign. Saturn there gives an older, more serious, more responsible partner and
usually a later marriage. Jupiter gives a well-educated, ethical, often
teaching or advisory partner. Venus gives an attractive, refined, comfort-loving
one. Mars gives an assertive, physically active, quick-tempered one. Mercury
gives a young-seeming, clever, talkative partner. Rahu gives a foreign,
cross-cultural or unconventional match. Ketu gives a detached, spiritual,
sometimes emotionally unavailable one. The Sun there can give a proud partner,
and classically stresses the marriage through ego.

The **placement of the 7th lord** says where the spouse comes from. In the 9th:
a different region, community or faith, or someone met through study or travel.
In the 10th: met through work. In the 12th: from far away, or met abroad. In
the 4th: from the same area, possibly a family connection.

## 2. Darakaraka — the Jaimini signature

In Jaimini astrology the **Darakaraka** is the planet with the *lowest* degree
among the seven chara karakas. It is the single most specific spouse indicator
in the system and is frequently more accurate than the 7th house.

The Darakaraka describes the spouse's core nature:

- **Sun** — proud, authoritative, a father-figure quality, political or
  governmental connection
- **Moon** — nurturing, moody, emotionally responsive, close to their mother
- **Mars** — competitive, physical, sharp-tongued, often in engineering,
  defence, sport or surgery
- **Mercury** — youthful, analytical, communicative, often in business,
  writing or teaching
- **Jupiter** — wise, generous, principled, often older or from a religious or
  academic background
- **Venus** — attractive, artistic, pleasure-loving, socially skilled
- **Saturn** — older, serious, hardworking, from a modest background,
  emotionally reserved
- **Rahu** — foreign, unconventional, from outside the expected circle

The **sign and house of the Darakaraka** add the detail. A Saturn Darakaraka in
the 10th describes a partner defined by their work and their sense of duty.

## 3. Upapada Lagna

The **Upapada Lagna (UL)** is calculated from the 12th house from the 2nd lord
and is the Jaimini point for the marriage itself. Its sign describes the
spouse's presentation and the marriage's public character. The **2nd house from
UL** is read for the marriage's durability — benefics there support the bond,
malefics stress it.

## 4. Navamsa (D9)

The D9 lagna and its lord describe who the person becomes inside a marriage,
and the D9 7th house refines the spouse description set by the D1. Where D1 and
D9 give different spouse types, the D9 usually describes the person actually
married and the D1 describes the type the person is attracted to. That gap —
attracted to one type, married to another — is a real and common pattern, and
naming it is often the most useful thing a reading does.

## 5. Venus and Jupiter as karakas

**Venus** is the natural karaka of the wife and of relationship pleasure
generally. Its condition — sign, dignity, aspects, combustion — describes the
quality of married life more than the spouse. **Jupiter** is the karaka of the
husband in a woman's chart, read the same way.

## The limits, stated clearly

Predictions of **physical appearance** are the weakest part of this system and
the part most often used to impress. Height, complexion and build attributions
are inherited from texts written in a specific region and century, and they do
not travel. Treat them as the least reliable output.

Predictions of **profession** are moderately reliable at the level of *category*
— analytical, physical, caring, commercial — and unreliable at the level of a
job title.

Predictions of **temperament and background** are the strongest, and are where a
reading earns its value.

## How to present it

Describe the type, name the indicators, and mark the confidence:

> "Your Darakaraka is Saturn in the 10th, and Saturn also sits in your 7th.
> Both point the same way: a partner older than you or older in manner,
> defined by their work, reserved with feelings but reliable with actions.
> Your 7th lord is in the 9th, so a different community or region is likely.
> That much is consistent across three indicators. I would not put weight on
> appearance — the chart is not good at that."
